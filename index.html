Meet Your Clinical Dietitian

I am Mehro Nisa Malik, an experienced Registered Clinical Dietitian passionate about bridging clinical diagnostics with real-life nutrition. I believe that healing begins on the plate, but it should never come at the expense of your joy, cultural food identity, or sanity.

Having managed complex acute and chronic conditions in top tertiary care hospitals, my philosophy rejects dangerous crash diets, detox teas, and impossible restrictions. Instead, we use personalized clinical biochemistry, hormone balancing, and sustainable meal design so your healthy lifestyle becomes effortless and lifelong.
